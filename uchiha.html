The Uchiha clan (うちは一族, Uchiha Ichizoku) is a prominent family in the Naruto series created by Masashi Kishimoto. Known for their exceptional skills in ninjutsu and their unique kekkei genkai, the Sharingan, the Uchiha clan plays a significant role in the storyline. The clan's history is marked by tragedy, rivalry, and a deep connection to the village of Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf Village). Key members of the Uchiha clan include Itachi Uchiha, Sasuke Uchiha, and Madara Uchiha, each contributing to the complex narrative of power, loyalty, and redemption within the series.
